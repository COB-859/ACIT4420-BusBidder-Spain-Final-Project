1. Price on a relative scale (the program) against a fixed scale
   60 requests: each of the 15 cities with each of the 4 departure periods, the whole week,
   with a budget at the median price of the city, under each of the 4 profiles: 240 rankings.
   Relative: cheapest offer left 100 points, dearest 0. Fixed: 0 points at the budget,
   100 points at half the budget or less, whatever the other offers cost.
   Rankings compared: 240
                                                              Relative   Fixed
   First two offers more than 0 and less than 1 point apart         83      73
   Order of the others changes when the last offer is removed       39      14
   The two scales recommend the same offer in 230 of 240 rankings.

2. How large must a weight be to decide the ranking? (the 70 percent limit)
   The same 60 requests. Price gets the weight shown; duration, departure and flexibility
   share the rest equally. Counted: rankings in which the cheapest offer wins.
   price weight 40%: the cheapest offer wins in  2 of 60
   price weight 50%: the cheapest offer wins in  8 of 60
   price weight 60%: the cheapest offer wins in 15 of 60
   price weight 70%: the cheapest offer wins in 20 of 60
   price weight 80%: the cheapest offer wins in 29 of 60
   price weight 90%: the cheapest offer wins in 44 of 60

   Yes or no was the program's rule before: 50 points for a refundable ticket, whatever
   it refunds. Now the 50 points are multiplied by the refund percentage.
   The 60 requests without a budget, 4 profiles: 240 rankings.
